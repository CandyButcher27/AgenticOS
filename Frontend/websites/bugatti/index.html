Bugatti stands for the pursuit of an idea so extreme that few dare to imagine it, let alone build it. Since 1909, every chassis to leave Molsheim has carried the same conviction — that engineering and art are not opposites, but the same discipline seen from two directions.

The Tourbillon is the clearest expression of that conviction yet: a naturally aspirated V16 paired with a hybrid architecture, wrapped in a body sculpted by wind and by hand. Nothing about it is accidental. Every surface has been argued over, every letter of the wordmark has been measured to the millimetre.
