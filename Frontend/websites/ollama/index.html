Start local.

Download Ollama and run any open model on your own machine in minutes. Your prompts and your data never leave your hardware.

ollama pull llama3.3

Scale cloud.

When you outgrow local hardware, move the same models to Ollama Cloud and keep the identical CLI and API surface — nothing in your code changes.

ollama run llama3.3 --cloud

Your data stays yours

Ollama runs models on your own hardware by default. Nothing you type is sent anywhere unless you explicitly opt into cloud execution. No training on your prompts, no telemetry on your conversations.

Frequently asked questions

What is Ollama?

Ollama is a tool for running open large language models on your own hardware, with a simple CLI, a local REST API, and an optional cloud tier when you need more compute than your machine can provide.

Which models can I run?

Ollama supports Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma, DeepSeek, and dozens of other open-weight model families. Browse the full catalog on the Models page and pull any of them with a single command.

Do you train on my data?

No. Local runs never leave your machine, and cloud runs are not used for training. See hello@ollama.com if you have questions about a specific workload.

Can I cancel Pro or Max anytime?

Yes. Plans are billed monthly with no long-term commitment, and you can downgrade to Free at any time from your account settings.

Is there a free trial for Max?

Pro includes everything most individual developers need. Reach out at hello@ollama.com to talk about a Max trial for your team.
